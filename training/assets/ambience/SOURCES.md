# Ambient audio sources

The short normalized WAV samples in this directory are committed for local experiments and repeatable pipeline checks. Each is mono, 16 kHz, PCM signed 16-bit. They are environmental negatives/backgrounds, not wake-word positives.

| File | Source recording | Author | License | Change made |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `city-rain-traffic-16k.wav` | [Urban Street on a Rainy Afternoon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Urban_Street_on_a_Rainy_Afternoon.flac) | Extemporalist | CC0 1.0 | First 20 seconds extracted and converted from FLAC to mono 16 kHz PCM S16LE. |
| `rain-thunder-16k.wav` | [Rain and thunder](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_and_thunder.ogg) | Caesar | Public domain | Full 19-second Ogg recording converted to mono 16 kHz PCM S16LE. |
| `wind-16k.wav` | [Howling wind](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Howling_wind.ogg) | Tvabutzku1234 | CC0 1.0 | First 30 seconds extracted and converted from Ogg Vorbis to mono 16 kHz PCM S16LE. |

The source pages contain the license statements and original metadata. These samples are starter coverage only; expand the corpus with diverse, properly licensed recordings and keep their provenance beside each imported file.
